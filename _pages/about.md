Lizichen Chen[📄](http://chainjackson.github.io/Chain.github.io/files/CV_Lizichen%20CHEN_Latex.pdf) is a Ph.D. student in Department of Engineering Mechanics, Zhejiang University, supervised by Prof. [Weiqiu Chen (陈伟球)](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=VJFMmQMAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao) and Prof. [C.W. Lim (林志华)](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=50FxROwAAAAJ&hl=en). During the doctoral program, he also received joint supervision from Prof. [Michel Destrade](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=EQAvBLgAAAAJ&hl=en), Prof. [Gangfeng Wang (王刚锋)](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=MB5AuWAAAAAJ&hl=en), and Prof. [Rongqiao Xu (徐荣桥)](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Rongqiao-Xu). Chen received his B.E. degree from Chu Kochen Honors College of Zhejiang University, China, in 2024, under the supervision of Prof. Weiqiu Chen (陈伟球). His research interests include **multiphysics coupling**, **contact mechanics**, and **surface physics**. He has published 13 papers with collaborators in applied mathematics and mechanics journals, including *J. Mech. Phys. Solids*, *Int. J. Solids Struct.*, and *Proc. R. Soc. A*. He has served as principal investigator or participant in three projects, including the NSFC Major Program and the NSFC Basic Research Project for Young Students. He was awarded the Young Elite Scientist Sponsorship Program (PhD Candidate) from CAST, the Qiushi Rising Star Program from Zhejiang University, and the Zhilun Xu Excellent Student of Mechanics Award from CSTAM. He serves as a reviewer for journals such as *J. Mech. Phys. Solids*. He has delivered invited talks at international conferences, including ICCES 2025 and 2026.

# Research
**Tactile Perception and Surface/Interface Intelligence (接触感知与表界面智能)**:
* Symplectic contact analysis (接触力学辛形式)
* Asymptotic contact analysis (接触力学渐近分析)
* Inverse problems in contact mechanics (接触力学反问题)
* Mechanics of puncture and fracture (穿刺与断裂力学)
* Tactile perception for robots (机器触觉感知)
